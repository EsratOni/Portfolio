Research process

How I research

Every project moves through five stages, using both qualitative and quantitative research. Each stage ends with something the team can use.

  1. 1

    Frame

    Turn a vague brief into clear research questions and the assumptions worth testing.

    Methods
    Stakeholder conversations, desk research, research plan
    Output
    Research questions and a plan
  2. 2

    Discover

    Hear from real people, in depth and at scale.

    Methods
    User interviews, user surveys, observation, competitive analysis
    Output
    Interview notes and survey data

    Kiosk study: 65 people surveyed, 5 interviewed

  3. 3

    Synthesise

    Find the patterns behind what people said and did.

    Methods
    Affinity mapping, qualitative coding, survey analysis, journey maps
    Output
    Key insights, each backed by evidence
  4. 4

    Design

    Turn insights into ideas the team can see and react to.

    Methods
    User flows, wireframes, prototypes in Figma
    Output
    A prototype ready to test
  5. 5

    Test and refine

    Check the design with real users before it is built.

    Methods
    Usability testing, task success, iteration
    Output
    Fixes and a clean developer handoff

In every study: informed consent, anonymised responses, and neutral questions that don't lead people to an answer.
